# Market data providers (checked 2026-10-12; recheck before relying on a limit)

| Provider | Plan | What it gives | Limits | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Finnhub | Free | `/quote`, `/stock/profile2`, `/stock/metric?metric=all`, `/company-news`, `/search`, `/stock/market-status`; WebSocket trades | 60 requests/min; 50 streamed symbols; one stream per key | Personal use only. No candles (`/stock/candle` is paid), no batch quotes, no volume on `/quote`. Quote `t` is not in the published schema. Metric returns have no as-of date. Answers BRK.B with BRK.A's profile. |
| Alpaca | Free (Basic) | SIP bars and trades for any range ending ≥15 min ago; IEX real time (latest trades/quotes, stream) | 200 requests/min; 30 streamed symbols | Keys in `APCA-API-KEY-ID` / `APCA-API-SECRET-KEY` headers. Bars: `limit` counts across all symbols per page. Real-time SIP costs $99/mo (Algo Trader Plus). |
| FRED | Free key | US economic series (`/series/observations` with `units` transforms, `/series`, `/series/release`, `/release/dates`) | 120 requests/min | Key as `api_key` query parameter; a bad key answers HTTP 400 naming `api_key`. Missing values are ".". Must show "This product uses the FRED® API but is not endorsed or certified by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis." Copyrighted third-party series (S&P, ICE BofA, Moody's, Freddie Mac, Michigan) need permission beyond personal use; the app uses government series only. |
| TradingView widgets | Free embeds | Charts, heatmap, screener, quotes | n/a | Delayed. Keep the attribution line. The page can't read data out of a widget. The screener embed takes only `market`, `defaultScreen` (most_capitalized, volume_leaders, unusual_volume, top_gainers, top_losers, most_volatile, high_dividend, above_52wk_high, below_52wk_low, monthly_high, monthly_low, ath, atl, overbought, oversold, outperforming_SMA50, underperforming_SMA50, earnings_this_week) and `defaultColumn` (overview, performance, valuation, dividends, margins, income_statement, balance_sheet, oscillators, moving_averages); its filters can't be preset, read or saved (lists from TradingView's widget builder, checked 2026-10-14 against typed wrappers). |

Request budget for the interactive heatmap: Finnhub 3 per stock (54 stocks ≈ 162 on a cold cache, cached 2 min to
24 h); Alpaca 6–7 per refresh for all stocks. Check scripts: `check:market-data`, `check:stream`, `check:fred`, `check:portfolio` (Alpaca daily bars; `check:alpaca` was removed with the heatmap's Alpaca code).
Portfolio backtests: Alpaca 1Day SIP bars, `adjustment=all`, since 2016, one call per symbol (~2,800 bars, one page), kept until the next session's bar is final; `end` must be at least 15 minutes ago or the free plan answers 403.
